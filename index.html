About Shree Laxmiji Lamination

Built on 25 years of uncompromised precision, manufacturing trust, and pan-India supply reliability.

Gujarat's Single-Window Flexible Packaging & Printing Partner

Shree Laxmiji Lamination is an Ahmedabad-based plastic roll printing and lamination company with over 25 years of continuous industry experience. Founded in 2000 and operating from Odhav — one of Gujarat's most prominent industrial hubs — we serve local enterprises and national commercial clients with a complete portfolio of high-precision plastic printing, lamination, and flexible packaging films.

From high-shrink sleeve bottle packaging to custom branded saree rolls, we deliver consistent gauge quality and vibrant print registration on every single meter. As a direct in-house manufacturer, we handle high-volume rotogravure printing and coordinate specialized multi-layer packaging solutions with complete reliability.
